How To Use

Get the most out of every scoop with this simple 3-step routine.

1

Mix

Mix 1 scoop with 250–300ml of cold water in a shaker bottle.

2

Time It Right

Drink 20–30 minutes before training to let the Tri-Stim energy blend kick in.

3

Mind The Clock

Due to the 300mg caffeine content, do not consume within 6 hours of bedtime.

Building Tolerance

New to high-stim pre-workouts? Start with a half scoop to assess your tolerance before moving to a full serving. Never exceed 1 serving in a 24-hour period.

Stacking Advice

C4 Ultimate can be stacked with a non-stimulant intra-workout (BCAAs/EAAs) or a stimulant-free creatine, but should not be combined with any other caffeinated pre-workout, energy drink or stimulant-based fat burner on the same day.

Warnings

Please read carefully before using C4 Ultimate.

Not intended for use by persons under 18 years of age. Do not use if pregnant or nursing.

Consult a licensed, qualified health care professional before using this product if you are taking any medication, are under a physician’s care, or have any pre-existing medical condition, including but not limited to high or low blood pressure, heart or cardiovascular disease, liver or kidney disease.

This product contains 300mg of caffeine per serving, comparable to about 3 cups of coffee. Do not use this product if you are sensitive to caffeine.

Do not exceed 1 serving in a 24-hour period. Do not combine with other caffeine or stimulant-containing products, including coffee, tea, soda or other energy drinks or supplements.

Discontinue use and consult a medical doctor immediately if you experience any adverse reaction to this product.

Keep out of reach of children. Store in a cool, dry place.
